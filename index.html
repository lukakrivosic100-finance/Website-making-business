A few useful answers

Questions? We've got you.

Is a domain included in the £22 plan?

One eligible standard domain is included, subject to availability and the domain and renewal terms agreed before ordering. Premium domains and some extensions may cost extra.

Can I use a domain I already own?

Yes, the £15/month option is designed for customers who already own and maintain their domain. We'll confirm whether it can be connected before work begins.

Can I request extra pages or features?

Yes. Tell us what you need and we'll confirm whether it fits the standard scope or needs a separate quote.

Can my website take online payments?

We can discuss supported hosted checkout or payment-link options, subject to your payment provider and merchant account. More advanced baskets, order management and integrations are quoted separately.

Can I update my website later?

Agreed basic updates are included within the service scope. Major redesigns, new functionality or substantial content changes may need a separate quote.

When will my website be ready?

The standard target is up to 5 days once we have your complete content and agreed the project scope. Domain delays, feedback and additional requirements can affect the launch date.
