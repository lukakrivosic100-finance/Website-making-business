Online shopping & payments

Want to take online payments?

We can discuss a product catalogue with payment links or a hosted checkout using a suitable provider such as myPOS, SumUp or Square.

Customers pay on the payment provider's secure page, not by entering card details into our basic static website.

How a starter shop works

  1. Your products appear on your website.
  2. The customer selects a product and follows a secure payment link.
  3. Your chosen payment provider processes the payment and handles its own fees.
  4. More complex baskets, order automation and stock management are quoted separately.

Availability depends on the provider, country, merchant account approval and product type. Setup, recurring platform charges, payment processing, refunds and chargebacks are not included in the displayed starter price unless stated in a written quote.

FAQ

Good questions, simple answers.

Is the domain really included?

Yes, the £22/month offer includes one eligible standard domain, subject to availability and the final plan terms. Premium domains and some extensions may cost extra.

Can I use my existing domain?

Usually, yes. Tell us about it before ordering so we can confirm the setup.

Can I request extra pages or features?

Of course. Let us know what you need and we'll give you a quote if it's outside the starter plan.

Can I use myPOS, SumUp or Square for my online shop?

Potentially, yes. We can connect supported online payment links or a hosted checkout, subject to your payment provider's eligibility and capabilities. A physical card reader alone does not create online checkout, and provider transaction fees are separate.

Can I make changes later?

Agreed basic updates are part of the service; major redesigns or additional features can be quoted separately.
