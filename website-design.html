Frequently asked questions

Questions to ask before choosing a website designer.

What is included in your £22 per month website?

The standard plan includes mobile-friendly website design, basic hosting, an eligible standard domain subject to availability and agreed terms, and basic SEO, photo gallery, opening hours and Maps integration as appropriate for your business. We confirm the exact requirements before you order.

Can I use a domain I already own?

Yes. Our £15/month option is for customers who already own and maintain their own domain. We can connect it without an extra domain-connection fee under the standard plan. Registration and renewal costs remain yours.

Will my website appear first on Google?

No website designer can promise that. Basic SEO helps search engines understand your site, but ranking depends on competition, relevance, links and many other factors. Google may need time to discover and index a new website.

Can you build a website for a café, salon or local service business?

Yes. Tell us what the business needs to show customers and we'll discuss a suitable standard site layout, such as a services section, image gallery, opening hours, contact details or booking links. Advanced features can require custom work.

Is an online shop with card payments included?

Not automatically. A standard small business website is different from a fully functioning online store. Payment checkout, customer accounts, stock management and custom integrations need separate requirements and may have extra development or provider costs.

Do I need to supply photos and business information?

We need accurate contact and business details, service descriptions and any content you want to feature. You can provide images and branding that you own or are authorised to use, and review all details before the website is published.
